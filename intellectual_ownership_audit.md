# Intellectual / problem ownership audit

Updated: 2026-09-28

This file is the persistent evidence layer for the master PhD database. It distinguishes **implementation ownership** (the student does the work) from **intellectual/problem ownership** (the student increasingly owns hypotheses, discriminating tests/models, interpretation, and what to try next).

## Status rubric

- **Demonstrated** — completed student thesis/project-chain evidence directly shows substantial and growing reasoning ownership.
- **Strong signal** — direct evidence supports autonomy/problem ownership, but longitudinal thesis-chain proof is incomplete.
- **Project-dependent** — evidence suggests ownership varies materially by thesis/project/facility role.
- **Unverified** — insufficient evidence; this is not a negative score and must not be treated as autonomy.
- **Weak** — direct evidence indicates a substantially task-defined/student-execution pattern. Use only with strong evidence.

Final-shortlist promotion should normally require Demonstrated or Strong signal, or explicitly carry an ownership-unverified/project-dependent caveat.

## Evidence standards / search procedure

For each serious candidate:
1. Find at least one completed PhD thesis/defense supervised by the PI; two when the group is large or heterogeneous.
2. Reconstruct the student's multi-year chain: problem → hypotheses/model → chosen experiment/analysis/intervention → result/surprise → interpretation → next decision.
3. Look for explicit contribution statements and acknowledgements about freedom, idea generation, experiment conception, method development and decision-making.
4. Separate **student contribution** from **group/collaboration contribution**.
5. Check whether later thesis chapters/papers are motivated by earlier findings.
6. Never infer intellectual ownership from prestige, publication count, facility access, hands-on intensity, or implementation ownership alone.
7. If evidence cannot establish the reasoning trajectory, record **Unverified**, not a guessed middle score.
