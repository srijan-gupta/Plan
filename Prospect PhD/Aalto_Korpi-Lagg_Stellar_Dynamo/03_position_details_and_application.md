## Project purpose
The advertised goal is to help identify and assess promising habitable exoplanets around solar-like magnetically active stars by building next-generation GPU-accelerated stellar-dynamo models and connecting them to exoplanet discovery and space-weather environments.

## Scientific / technical role
The doctoral researcher will model and interpret stellar dynamo mechanisms and magnetic activity using fully compressible MHD in global and local frameworks, together with physics-informed ML and coronal/wind modelling.

### Advertised responsibilities
1. Develop and implement data-driven differential-rotation constraints in global dynamo models, including relaxation terms in the Navier-Stokes equation, calibrated against solar/stellar observations.
2. Design, run, and analyse large-scale global solar/stellar dynamo simulations that capture small- and large-scale dynamos together, at resolutions requiring LUMI-class resources.
3. Use observational constraints for model selection/pruning, run the best matches across multiple stellar-activity cycles, and prepare datasets for ML analysis.
4. Couple global and local radiative-MHD models; contribute to GPU acceleration of radiative transfer and high-cadence data extraction; use ML to isolate active-region progenitors and investigate eruptive conditions.
5. Interface with SWMF modules (AWSoM/IA) for stellar corona/wind modelling driven by dynamo-generated surface magnetic maps.
6. Publish/disseminate results and contribute open-science data/code.
7. Participate in teaching according to department practice.
