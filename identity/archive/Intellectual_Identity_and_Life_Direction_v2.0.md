# Intellectual Identity & Life Direction

**Version:** 1.5

## Mission

This document is the explicit model of how I understand myself, make
important life decisions, and continuously refine those decisions over
decades.

It is **not** about defending a career choice.

It is about preserving the reasoning that produced that choice.

Career directions are outputs. This document is the reasoning engine.

# Gospel (Read Before Every Edit)

Whenever the document or discussion loses direction, return here.

## What this document is

-   A model of my intellectual identity.
-   A model of my life decision process.
-   A record of how evidence changed my understanding of myself.
-   A living document that should remain useful even if every current
    career conclusion changes.

## What this document is NOT

-   A diary.
-   A career plan.
-   A justification of previous decisions.
-   A collection of interests.

## Core Principles

1.  Preserve **transformations**, not conclusions.
2.  Preserve **why**, not merely **what**.
3.  Identity is more fundamental than career.
4.  Evidence updates identity; identity updates the framework; the
    framework produces hypotheses.
5.  Optimize for truth, not consistency.
6.  Stable layers require strong evidence to change.
7.  Dynamic layers should evolve freely.
8.  Never begin from conclusions---always begin from observations.
9.  Every important realization should remain understandable years
    later.

## Permanent Working Structure

Every discussion follows this order:

Current Document Location ↓ Current Objective ↓ One Question / One
Observation ↓ Identity Discovery ↓ Framework Update (if any) ↓
Hypothesis Update (if any) ↓ Document Update ↓ Next TODO

Never skip steps.

# Methodology for Building This Document

This document is being constructed using the same standards that should be applied to good research.

## Method

1. Observe experiences.
2. Separate observation from interpretation.
3. Infer identity traits only when supported by multiple independent observations.
4. Update the decision framework only after identity has changed.
5. Allow career hypotheses to emerge from the framework.
6. Preserve all important transformations and revisions.

This prevents hindsight bias and reduces the risk of constructing a narrative to justify previous decisions.

## Why This Methodology?

Identity is latent.

It is revealed through repeated interaction with reality, not by introspection alone.

Therefore the document grows from evidence upward rather than from conclusions downward.

# Implementation of the Cooling Principle

The document deliberately matures in layers.

## Layer 1 — Architecture

Changes only when a fundamental representational flaw is discovered.

Expected stability:

## Layer 2 — Identity

Changes only after multiple independent observations contradict an existing belief.

Expected stability:

## Layer 3 — Decision Framework

Updated only when identity changes sufficiently.

Expected stability:
High.

## Layer 4 — Evidence Register

Continuously grows.

Rarely rewritten.

Expected stability:
Medium.

## Layer 5 — Current Hypotheses

Expected to evolve frequently as new evidence accumulates.

Expected stability:
Low.

## Layer 6 — Concrete Decisions

Masters, PhD, employers, research groups.

These are expected to change the most and should never be mistaken for identity.

The goal is that the deeper layers become increasingly stable while the outer layers remain adaptable.
